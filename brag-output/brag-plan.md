# Brag plan: EuroDrive Lead Desk

## Rubric
- **What is it?** A sales-lead desk for a car dealer group. Showroom enquiries land live in a scored inbox, and every lead opens with the next step ready.
- **Who is it for?** Car-sales reps, plus their managers and VPs. Reps learn which lead to work next and how. Managers steer the team. VPs see the whole funnel.
- **What sets it apart?** It's live end to end (showroom form → SSE toast in the inbox), every score is explained, each customer's channel and hours are respected, and an AI next-best-action falls back to rules when the AI is unavailable.
- **Most impressive claim:** it was built in one day (first commit 30 Sep 10:47, last 1 Oct 11:27) from 20 feature specs, with up to 6 Claude agents building in parallel and 837 tests.
- **Visual hook:** a real buyer clicks "Send enquiry" on the showroom, then a real "New pool lead: Marta Vogel" toast pops in the manager's inbox.
- **Real UI shown:** showroom enquiry form, inbox table and live toast, Marta's lead detail (score card, channel prefs, AI next best action), pipeline kanban, VP dashboard. All of it was captured from the running local apps.
- **Tone:** default. Punchy and clean, in the app's own dark navy with brand blue.
- **Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.

## Angle
Follow one real lead, Marta, from the click to the sales desk. Then pull back to the twist: the whole thing was built in a day.
